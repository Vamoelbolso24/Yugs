**Thank you for playing Yugs: Playground Dueling!**

Yugs is a free fan project. It has no relation to Konami, Shueisha, Nintendo or The Pokémon Company. If you enjoy the game, please support Yu-Gi-Oh! by buying official Konami products.

**About the Pokémon cards:** some of their artwork comes from free image websites, and I tried to credit each artist on the card itself. If you are an artist and I credited you incorrectly, or you would like your image taken down, email us at **support@playgrounddueling.com** and we will fix it right away.

**Want to help?** You can donate artwork for the Pokémon cards: send it to **support@playgrounddueling.com** and we will credit you on the card.
