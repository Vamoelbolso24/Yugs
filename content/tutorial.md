## Welcome to Yugs: Playground Dueling

Yugs is a free Yu-Gi-Oh! duel website with its own collection, packs, ranked ladder and some custom cards. It is in **beta**: expect some bugs and the odd maintenance day. If something breaks, please tell us (see "Found a bug?" at the end).

## Getting started

- Pick one of the three starter decks: Yugi, Kaiba or Joey. It becomes your first deck and its cards go into your collection.
- You also get **5 free packs**. Open them from the Packs screen whenever you like.
- Your active deck is the one you duel with. Change it in the Deck editor.

## Dueling

- **Find a duel (ranked)** pairs you with a player of similar rating. The longer you wait, the wider the search; after a minute anyone can be your opponent.
- **Duel a friend** makes an invite link to send to someone. Friendly duels are just for fun: no coins, no ELO, and they don't count toward packs.
- **The clock:** in online duels each player has **3 minutes for the whole duel**, plus **3 seconds back after every decision**. Your clock only runs while the game is waiting for you. If it reaches 0, you lose.
- **Leaving:** if you close the duel for more than 2 minutes, you lose.
- **Responding to your opponent:** click a glowing card to activate it. The menu next to your name sets how often the game stops to ask if you want to respond: "when something happens" (recommended), "always" or "never".

## ELO and Standings

- Everyone starts at **1000 ELO**. Ranked wins raise it and losses lower it; beating a stronger player gives more, losing to a weaker one costs more. Surrendering counts as a loss.
- The **Standings** page shows the top 100, your own place, and a search to find any player's rank. You appear there after your first ranked duel.

## Coins and packs

- Each **ranked win gives 2 coins**.
- A pack costs **10 coins**, and you need **10 finished ranked duels since your last pack** (a duel you surrender doesn't count for you).
- You can buy **2 packs**, then the shop rests until **00:00 UTC on the third day** after your second pack (21:00 the day before in Uruguay). Example: buy your second pack on Monday at any time, and you can buy again from Thursday 00:00 UTC.
- Every card in a pack rolls its rarity: Common 75%, Rare 16%, Super Rare 6%, Ultra Rare 2.5%, Secret Rare 0.5%.
- **Bad luck protection:** if a pack was all Commons, the last card of your next pack is Rare or better.
- The packs in the shop can change every Monday at 00:00 UTC.
- You can own up to 3 copies of a card; extra copies are marked "Already 3".

## The deck editor

- Main Deck 40 to 60 cards, Extra Deck up to 15, and up to 3 copies of a card.
- **Banlist:** a small number on a card means it is limited: 1 = Limited, 2 = Semi-Limited, 0 = Forbidden. Decks that break the banlist can't duel until fixed.
- **On a phone:** tap a card to read it, then tap it again to add it to (or take it out of) your deck.
- Use **Sort** and **Filters** to find cards by name or text, frame, attribute, type, level, ATK, DEF and more. All filters combine.

## Card pictures

The official card art is not stored on Yugs. Cards always show a frame with their name, stats and text, so you can always play. You can add the pictures yourself; they are saved only in your browser.

**On a computer, with EDOPro installed**

- Open **Card images** on the main menu and press **Choose pics folder**. Pick the folder called "pics" inside your EDOPro folder (often called ProjectIgnis).
- Some pictures missing? EDOPro only downloads a picture the first time it shows that card. Open EDOPro's deck editor, look through the cards you want, then choose the folder again here.

**On a phone, or without EDOPro**

- Every card has an **ID number**. You see it at the bottom of the card details when you look at a card.
- Find a picture of the card and save it named after its ID, for example `46986414.jpg` for Dark Magician (`.jpg` or `.png`).
- Open **Card images**, press **Choose single pictures** and pick those files.

Custom cards always show their art, on every device.

## Found a bug?

Press **Report a bug** (on the main page, or at the top of the screen during a duel). Tell us what happened, what you clicked and which card was involved; we attach the screen you were on and, in a duel, the last lines of the duel log. Screenshots help a lot: send them to **support@playgrounddueling.com**.
