## Rules

Yugs: Playground Dueling is a free fan game. Keep it fun for everyone:

- **Be respectful.** No insults, harassment, hate speech, threats or spam, in chat or in your username.
- **No cheating.** Don't use bots, scripts or bugs to win, earn coins or open packs. Found a bug? Press **Report a bug**; reporting it is never a problem, abusing it is.
- **One account per person.** No extra accounts to collect free packs or coins, and no arranged wins between accounts to raise ELO.
- **Don't leave duels on purpose.** Running out your clock or disconnecting to annoy your opponent counts as a loss, and doing it again and again can get you banned.
- **Keep your account to yourself.** Don't share or sell it, and don't pretend to be an admin.
- **Don't post other people's private information**, or links to harmful websites.

If someone breaks the rules, admins can delete messages, mute players in chat, or ban accounts for a few days or for good. A ban counts any duel in progress as a loss. If you think a decision was wrong, email **support@playgrounddueling.com** from the email of your account.

## Privacy

**What we keep about you**

- **Your account:** your username, your email and your password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it, not even the admins.
- **Your game:** your collection, decks, coins, packs opened, duel results and ELO. Your username, ELO and win/loss record appear in the Standings.
- **What you write:** chat messages (everyone sees the latest 100; only the latest 1,000 are kept, so admins can check reports of bad behavior) and bug reports (only admins see them, together with the screen you were on and the last lines of the duel log).

**Your email** is only used to send your sign-up code and password-reset codes, and to answer you if you write to us. Other players never see it. We don't send newsletters or ads, and we never sell or share it. The emails are sent through the email service Resend.

**Cookies and your browser:** Yugs sets one cookie, only to keep you logged in. There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics. Card pictures you load from your own folder stay in your browser; they are never uploaded.

**Your internet address (IP)** is only held in the server's memory for a short time to stop password guessing and spam. It is not saved.

**Deleting your account:** email **support@playgrounddueling.com** from the email of your account and we will delete it and everything linked to it.

Yugs is meant for players aged 13 and older.

This page may change as the game grows; big changes will be announced in the news.
